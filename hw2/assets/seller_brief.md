## Why I chose Nexa

I want to help Nexa find sales targets because I care about water, yet water is often invisible. Nexa helps customers gain visibility into how water is moving inside their infrastructure and monitor and protect their assets.

## Product and customer problem

Nexa sells a full-system water sensing and management solution: sensing hardware, connected equipment, a web/mobile platform, alerts, reporting, and customer-success support. Nexa describes the goal as giving buildings visibility into water systems and helping teams protect property, manage plumbing and hydronic systems, and save water and cost. [Hardware](https://www.nexaplatform.com/hardware) [Software](https://www.nexaplatform.com/software)

The product should therefore be understood as a business-to-business infrastructure solution whose value may include:

- making water movement inside buildings observable through flow, meter, temperature, pressure, and leak sensing;
- detecting leaks and abnormal usage, with alerts and automated reports;
- supporting remote monitoring and control through web and mobile applications; and
- helping operators protect property, manage domestic hot water, hydronic, and cooling-tower systems, and reduce water waste.

These capabilities are stated on Nexa’s product pages. Performance claims and customer outcomes should still be treated as Nexa’s own marketing claims unless independently validated.

## What it can and cannot do

### Supported use stated by Nexa

- Detect moisture, abnormal flow, unexpected water activity, and selected temperature/pressure conditions.
- Alert staff by phone, text, and email, provide reports, and support remote monitoring.
- Automatically close compatible water supplies when a leak is detected and remotely control compatible equipment.
- Monitor domestic hot water, hydronic loops, cooling towers, water meters, and building water systems.
- Support properties including hospitality, multi-family residential, education, commercial real estate/offices, data centers, and healthcare.

### Boundaries that must be confirmed

The website does not establish that Nexa:

- can monitor every type of water infrastructure or every building system;
- replaces an existing SCADA, BMS, CMMS, metering, or facility-management system;
- provides municipal water/wastewater utility monitoring, industrial process-water monitoring, or agricultural monitoring;
- guarantees that leaks, losses, outages, or damage will be prevented; or
- provides emergency response, engineering certification, or regulatory compliance services.

The sales agent should not claim that Nexa performs any of these functions unless a source confirms it. Monitoring and visibility should not be equated with physical control, guaranteed prevention, or emergency response.

## Constraints and qualification questions

Before recommending a prospect, the agent should look for evidence about:

- whether the prospect owns or operates water infrastructure;
- asset scale, number of sites, and geographic footprint;
- the prospect’s current measurement and monitoring systems;
- whether the site can support installation, connectivity, power, and maintenance;
- data, cybersecurity, privacy, and procurement requirements;
- regulatory or reporting obligations;
- who owns the problem and budget (for example, facilities, utilities, operations, engineering, sustainability, or asset management); and
- whether the prospect has a visible business need such as leakage, aging assets, water scarcity, service reliability, maintenance cost, or resilience planning.

## Information the sales-targeting agent should collect

For each prospective target, collect:

1. Company name, website, headquarters, and operating locations.
2. Industry and type of water infrastructure owned or operated.
3. Evidence of water-intensive operations or distributed/complex assets.
4. Evidence of leakage, aging infrastructure, water scarcity, resilience, compliance, maintenance, or visibility needs.
5. Current monitoring, metering, controls, or asset-management tools, if public.
6. Relevant decision-makers and departments.
7. Trigger event, such as a capital project, sustainability goal, incident, expansion, regulation, or public water-risk commitment.
8. Why Nexa may fit, stated cautiously and tied to evidence.
9. Unknowns that require discovery before outreach.
