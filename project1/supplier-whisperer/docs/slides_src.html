The problem: the supplier is awake when you are not

  • Hardware engineers bring products to mass production with suppliers 12 hours ahead.
  • Every question is a 7 am or 9 pm call, or a lost day per reply cycle.
  • Product delays cost 5.25% of market value on average (Hendricks & Singhal).
  • Real story: paying one supplier after qualification took 3 weeks: payment, bank and address details clarified one email a day. Momentum killed.

My baseline, 5 years in NPI

  • 3–5 overseas suppliers per program
  • ~10 supplier emails a week at peak, 40% pure technical questions
  • 3–5 early/late calls a week
  • 6–10 h/week outside 9–5 = $15.6k–26k per engineer per year

What's new

Today's options

  • Gmail Gemini, Copilot, Superhuman, ChatGPT: draft for a human to send
  • Lindy: can send, but can't tell "draft angle" from "agree to a price"
  • AgentMail: who may email and injection scanning, not what to say
  • Procurement startups: after the PO, for buyers, not engineers

What we do differently

  • Commitment-aware: 11 code checks on every reply
  • Grounded: answers only from the shareable spec; confidential never reaches a model
  • Asks, doesn't stall: "Wei will review" + the questions he needs answered
  • Walk first, then run: a week of shadow mode on the user's own data, then autonomy one category at a time

How the agents work

📨 Signed webhook / Gmail→ 🛡️ Sender + injection gates→ 🧭 Manager→ 📏 Spec check→ 📚 Context→ ✍️ Email agent→ ✅ Compliance→ ⚖️ Decision (code)

Purple = gpt-6-luna leaf calls (JSON out, no tools that act). Gray = deterministic code. Live view: the Manager is thinking; each handoff is logged.

Safety: code decides, not the model

  • $0 Hidden prompt injection quarantined before any model reads it
  • $0 Lookalike domain asking for new bank details: blocked
  • checks 11 code checks on every reply: no prices, quantities, dates, commitment words, links, files, confidential terms
  • spec "The probe is copper" vs. stainless: caught and asked
  • limits Budget caps, loop guard, kill switch, DKIM/DMARC on real mail, Low-risk shadow mode
