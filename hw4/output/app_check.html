Check 2 — A category question repaints the product grid

Asked from a product detail page: “what hoodies do you have?”

What this proves: Asking a category question in chat drove the page, not just the conversation. The site moved the shopper to the storeroom, replaced the 102-item grid with the eight matches, and labelled it “Handsome Dan found 8 matches for hoodies.” Every card is a real catalogue row with its own image, name and price, and every one opens its full product page when clicked.

Bug found by this test. The question was asked from a product page, and at first nothing appeared: the code checked whether the path started with /products, which is true of /products/benjamin-franklin-fleece-jacket too — so it set the results and left the shopper on a page with no grid. Now an exact match, and the screenshot above was taken after the fix.

Check 3 — Usability feature from Problem 9: Recent Products Viewed

Viewed three products, then returned to the storeroom.

What this proves: The site remembers what a signed-in shopper looked at and shows it back on return, most recent first — Boola Boola T Shirt is first because it was opened last. The strip sits above the full 102-item grid, so a returning customer picks up where they left off instead of searching again.

Views are recorded for signed-in shoppers only; a guest sees no strip and nothing is stored. The Clear link lets a shopper erase the history.
